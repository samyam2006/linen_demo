Our Story

We got tired
of plastic.

ANDRO began with a simple, uncomfortable question: why is the fabric closest to a man's body almost always made of plastic?

The Beginning

Founded on
a first principle.

We spent months reading labels. Nearly every "premium" pair of men's underwear on the market — even the expensive ones — was built on polyester, nylon and elastane. Synthetics chosen because they're cheap and stretchy, not because they're good for you.

So we went the other way. We sourced the finest European flax, engineered a linen weave soft enough to wear every day, and built a brand around a single promise: nothing synthetic, ever, against the part of you that matters most.

What We Stand For

Three rules.
No exceptions.

01

Natural, always

One fibre, honestly sourced. If it isn't 100% traceable European linen, it doesn't carry our name.

02

Built to last

Linen outlives synthetics by years and gets better with age. We make things you keep, not things you replace.

03

Radically honest

No inflated health claims, no greenwashing. We tell you exactly what our product is — and what it isn't.
