The Science

Heat is the
enemy.

Your body keeps its most sensitive biology a few degrees below core temperature — for a reason. The fabric you wear all day either helps that, or fights it. Here's why we chose linen.

01

Cooler by design

The male body is engineered to keep the groin slightly below core temperature. Trapped heat is the single most avoidable disruptor of that system — and synthetics trap the most.

02

Linen breathes

Flax fibre is hollow and highly moisture-wicking. It moves air and pulls sweat away from the skin, keeping you measurably cooler and drier than cotton or polyester.

03

Nothing synthetic

No polyester, no nylon, no elastane, no plastic microfibres against your skin. Just a single natural fibre that's been trusted for four thousand years.

The Fibre

One fibre.
Four thousand
years of proof.

Linen is the oldest textile known to man — and still the most breathable natural fibre we have. It conducts heat away from the body, absorbs up to 20% of its weight in moisture before feeling damp, and dries fast. It's naturally antibacterial, hypoallergenic and gets softer every time you wash it.

Where polyester insulates and holds moisture like a plastic bag, linen does the opposite. That's not marketing — it's the physics of the fibre.

A note on claims: ANDRO makes underwear, not medicine.
