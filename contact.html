Before You Ask

Common questions.

Is linen really comfortable every day?

Yes. Our weave is finished to be soft from the first wear and only gets softer. It breathes far better than cotton and never feels clingy like synthetics.

How do I care for it?

Machine wash cold, tumble dry low or hang. Linen is tougher than it looks and improves with every cycle.

What's your return policy?

Try a pair for 30 days. If it isn't the most comfortable underwear you own, send it back for a full refund — no questions.

Do you really make health claims?

No. We make breathable, natural-fibre underwear that keeps you cooler and drier. We don't claim it treats or changes any medical or hormonal condition. See The Science for the honest version.
